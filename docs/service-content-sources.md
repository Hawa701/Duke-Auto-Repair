# Service content sources

Reviewed October 18, 2026. The new pages use original service descriptions informed by the primary sources below. Sources support general vehicle-care information; they do not establish Duke Auto's equipment, pricing, turnaround times, certifications or brand affiliations.

The approved brake design and supplied brake copy are retained through the shared `components/service-detail.tsx` layout. Content lives in `lib/service-details.ts`; routes and related-service links come from `lib/services.ts`.

| Service | Sources | Editorial focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oil change | [AAA oil-change guidance](https://ww2.aaa.com/autorepair/articles/how-often-should-you-change-your-oil), [AAA manufacturer specifications](https://www.northway.aaa.com/magazine/news/how-often-should-you-change-engine-oil) | Correct oil specifications and vehicle-specific schedules; no universal change interval. |
| BG fluid services | [BG service families](https://www.bgprod.com/services/), [BG engine maintenance](https://www.bgprod.com/services/engine/), [BG transmission service](https://www.bgprod.com/services/transmission/), [BG brake-fluid service](https://www.bgprod.com/services/brakes/), [BG maintenance limitation](https://www.bgprod.com/reference/pxt2-2/) | Applicable preventive maintenance; no promise that a fluid exchange repairs a mechanical fault or provides BG warranty coverage. |
| Battery services | [AAA battery warning signs](https://ww1.aaa.com/autorepair/articles/know-when-to-replace-the-car-battery-8-key-warning-signs), [AAA battery and charging checks](https://autoclubsouth.aaa.com/automotive/auto_maintenance_text.aspx?section=Batteries), [VARTA battery-management requirements](https://www.varta-automotive.com/en-gb/knowledge/articles/article-details/the-right-coding-of-the-battery-energy-management-system-for-battery-replacement) | Testing, connections, charging and vehicle-specific replacement procedures. |
| Suspension and steering | [Monroe symptoms](https://www.monroe.com/technical-resources/shocks-101/symptoms-worn-shock-struts.html), [Monroe leakage assessment](https://aftermarket.monroe.com/technical-resources/servicegrams/weepage-vs-leakage-shocks-struts.html), [Virginia safety checks](https://vsp.virginia.gov/public-safety-programs-center/vehicle-safety-inspection/) | Inspect the cause of handling concerns; no automatic replacement based on mileage or minor weepage. |
| Air conditioning | [DENSO system operation](https://www.denso-am.eu/gr/news/as-the-seasons-change-air-conditioning-system-service-should-be-a-priority-for-workshop-and-motorist-alike), [DENSO diagnosis](https://www.densoautoparts.com/reference-ac-compressor-troubleshooting/), [AAA A/C symptoms](https://www.acg.aaa.com/connect/blogs/4c/auto/how-to-know-your-cars-ac-needs-repair?sf203619582=1) | Distinguish cooling, leakage, airflow and control faults; confirm refrigerant compatibility. No claim of universal refrigerant coverage. |
| Heating | [DENSO heater cores](https://www.denso-am.eu/products/ac-engine-cooling/heater-cores), [DENSO thermostats](https://www.denso-am.eu/products/ac-engine-cooling/thermostats), [DENSO thermal systems](https://www.denso-am.eu/products/ac-engine-cooling), [AAA heater/defroster guidance](https://exchange.aaa.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/AAA-Car-Care-Maintenance.pdf) | Conventional coolant-based heating, airflow and controls. Avoid implying every electric or hybrid heating system works the same way. |
| Wheel alignment | [Michelin alignment and balancing](https://www.michelinman.com/auto/auto-tips-and-advice/tire-maintenance/wheel-alignment-wheel-balancing), [Michelin alignment angles](https://www.michelinman.com/auto/auto-tips-and-advice/tire-maintenance/routine-tire-care-tips), [AAA alignment and suspension](https://www.acg.aaa.com/connect/blogs/5c/auto/vehicle-wheel-alignment-and-suspension) | Manufacturer specifications; alignment differs from balancing and cannot fix worn components. |
| Check-engine diagnostics | [EPA check-engine guidance](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P100MHPD.TXT), [EPA OBD information](https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P1009Z15.TXT), [EPA readiness guidance](https://www.epa.gov/state-and-local-transportation/vehicle-emissions-inspection-and-maintenance-im-policy-and-technical) | Codes guide diagnosis; targeted tests and post-repair checks matter. A flashing light merits prompt attention and minimized driving. |
| State safety and emissions | [Virginia State Police inspection procedure](https://vsp.virginia.gov/public-safety-programs-center/vehicle-safety-inspection/), [VSP inspection FAQs](https://vsp.virginia.gov/public-safety-programs-center/vehicle-inspection-faqs/), [Virginia DMV emissions eligibility](https://www.dmv.virginia.gov/vehicles/registration/emissions), [Virginia DEQ Air Check](https://www.deq.virginia.gov/air-energy/vehicle-emissions-air-check) | Separate safety and emissions requirements, eligibility and readiness. The page links directly to current official requirements and makes no passing guarantee. |
